The fourteen files in `V22/` and `R4/` are original instance files from McMenemy, P., Veerapen, N., Adair, J., and Ochoa, G. (2019), *Rigorous Performance Analysis of State-of-the-art TSP Heuristic Solvers*, University of Stirling. They come from the RUE, NETGEN and MORPH families.
